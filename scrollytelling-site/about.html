The Beginning

Born from
a single idea

Ensemble was founded on a simple conviction: the best-dressed man in the room is also the most comfortable. Not despite it. Because of it.

We set out to build a co-ord set so considered in its cut and so fine in its fabric that getting dressed would never feel like effort again.

The Fabric

Italian linen.
Nothing else.

Every piece begins in a family-run mill in Emilia-Romagna. The linen is stonewashed by hand until it reaches the exact weight, drape, and softness we demand.

The result is a fabric that gets better with every wash and feels more like a second skin with each wear.

Our Values

Three principles.
No exceptions.

Craft

Each set is cut by hand in a small atelier. Pattern, placement, and proportion are reviewed at every step. We do not rush the process.

Fabric

Italian stonewashed linen. Pre-washed micro-pleated fabric. We source only from mills that share our obsession with hand, weight, and breathability.

Silhouette

Relaxed without being sloppy. Tailored without being stiff. The Ensemble silhouette is designed to look as good at a restaurant as it does on a beach.

The Pleated Collection

dimension

Our Ardoise pleated set extends the Ensemble language into a new texture. Micro-pleated fabric gives structure without stiffness and catches the light with every movement.

It is the same silhouette. An entirely different feeling.
